The studio

Built on how cars are engineered.

DrivenByDetail is an independent vehicle enhancement studio in North Harbour, Eastbourne.

I combine professional detailing and paint-protection work with more than a decade across vehicle mechanics and auto-electrics. That background means I approach each car with an understanding of how it is built — not simply how it looks.

From paint correction and ceramic coatings to PPF and neatly integrated dash cameras, every job is assessed individually and never rushed.

Do you offer mobile detailing?+

Yes. Our self-sufficient van carries its own water and power, so we can work at homes and businesses across Eastbourne and the BN20–BN27 areas.

Which services happen in the studio?+

Paint correction, ceramic coatings, new-car packages, PPF and vinyl wrapping are completed in our controlled Eastbourne studio.

How much does detailing cost?+

It depends on size, condition and the service. We confirm the price before booking — no extra work without your approval.

How do I book?+

Send an enquiry with photos. Once confirmed, a 50% deposit secures your date and the balance is paid on completion.
